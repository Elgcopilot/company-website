## The engineering challenge

A race machine's tail light is a safety system: at closing speeds on a wet track, the rider behind must see braking and running states instantly. The S63 project's Tera tail needed a control board that drives high-brightness LEDs reliably through vibration, rain, and noisy 12V vehicle power.

## What ELG engineered

ELG designed the Tera tail back-light control board — power conditioning for vehicle transients, high-current LED drive stages, and control behavior tuned for maximum conspicuity at speed. Trackside photos show the lit tail assembly and the finished board installed on the machine.

## Why it matters to your business

Small rugged control boards are ELG's bread and butter: power-tolerant design, bright outputs, and installation that survives the field. If your product needs to be seen — or to signal — in harsh conditions, this is the pattern.
