## The engineering challenge

Race engineering decisions happen under time pressure. The S63 program needed vehicle data, GPS, video, and diagnostics to remain visible to the pit team despite vibration, heat, and crowded trackside connectivity.

## What ELG engineered

ELG integrated the vehicle-side acquisition hardware, embedded software, communications, and monitoring interfaces into one trackside telemetry platform. The system was installed, tested, and supported during live competition sessions.

## Why it matters to your business

The same architecture applies anywhere a moving machine must report reliable data through difficult conditions: capture locally, preserve context, communicate resiliently, and give operators one clear view.
