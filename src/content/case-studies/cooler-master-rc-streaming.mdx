## The engineering challenge

Cooler Master wanted a booth experience students would queue for: drive a real RC car from a gaming rig, watching a live camera feed — with zero visible lag and hardware that survives a full day of enthusiastic drivers.

## What ELG engineered

ELG built a remote-controlled car with an integrated streaming camera feeding a gaming-rack monitor in real time, paired with a steering-rig driving setup. The installation ran all day at a university event, blending physical play with live video feedback.

## Why it matters to your business

Low-latency video over wireless in a crowded hall is the same engineering muscle behind ELG's motorsport streaming. This project shows the team delivering experiential technology that simply works in front of a live audience.
