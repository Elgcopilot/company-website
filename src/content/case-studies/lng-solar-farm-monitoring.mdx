## The engineering challenge

An LNG gas and solar farm operator needed real-time visibility into gas usage, solar production, and environmental conditions — across facilities where sending an engineer for every anomaly is expensive and slow.

## What ELG engineered

ELG developed an integrated monitoring system using IoT data collection across both facilities, feeding efficient remote management. Inverter, meteorological, and measurement data flow into operational views that keep both plants at optimal performance with fewer site visits.

## Why it matters to your business

Mixed-asset energy sites are the classic harsh-environment IoT problem: outdoor exposure, long distances, and data you cannot afford to lose. This deployment shows ELG's gateway-to-dashboard pattern working on live energy infrastructure.
