## The engineering challenge

Embedded engineering cannot be learned from slides. Burapha University's Embedded System Engineering department needed affordable, durable, curriculum-ready hardware that gives every student real hands-on development experience.

## What ELG engineered

ELG developed and delivered a custom Embedded Linux board as an educational tool — with manuals, instruction, and training that slot directly into coursework and build practical embedded development skills.

## Why it matters to your business

A company universities trust to teach embedded systems is a company you can trust to build them. This project reflects ELG's depth at the board and OS level — the foundation under every larger ecosystem we ship.
