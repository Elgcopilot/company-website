## The engineering challenge

Agricultural AI cannot depend on a datacenter connection. KMITL needed a board that runs inference in the field while also serving as the IoT gateway for sensors, actuators, and the cloud platform.

## What ELG engineered

ELG developed and delivered a custom Embedded Linux board for AI workloads in agriculture — an IoT gateway integrating sensors, actuators, and cloud connectivity in a single field-deployable design.

## Why it matters to your business

Edge AI projects fail at the board level long before they fail at the model level. This collaboration proves ELG designs production-grade AI-capable hardware with the full software stack to support it.
