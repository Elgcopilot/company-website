## The engineering challenge

A smart-home brand needed more than a prototype: a manufacturable Zigbee gateway that pairs cleanly with Home Assistant, Echo Plus, Hue, and SmartThings — at a retail cost structure and with packaging ready for Amazon and Shopee.

## What ELG engineered

ELG took the product from design through prototype confirmation to manufacturing: a Zigbee 3.0 USB dongle gateway bridging Zigbee devices to computers and IoT networks, with branding, paid advertising support, and domestic plus international marketplace placement.

## Why it matters to your business

Most engineering houses stop at the prototype. Bee-Sync proves ELG can carry a connected product all the way to retail shelves — hardware, firmware, manufacturing handoff, and go-to-market assets in one engagement.
