## The engineering challenge

The light assembly needed compact electronics that could tolerate vehicle power, drive a long high-brightness LED form consistently, and fit the product's mechanical constraints.

## What ELG engineered

ELG designed the control board, power interface, connectors, and embedded behavior, then integrated and validated the electronics with the complete Tera tail-light assembly.

## Why it matters to your business

Products become reliable when electronics, firmware, wiring, and mechanics are designed together. This project shows that complete integration in a compact automotive subsystem.
