## The engineering challenge

Race teams need useful information while cars are moving, not after the session. The system had to collect vehicle and driver signals across multiple cars and keep engineers informed in a demanding trackside environment.

## What ELG engineered

ELG integrated data acquisition, embedded processing, connectivity, and monitoring dashboards into a trackside system used during Toyota racing operations. The field work included installation, configuration, and live engineering support.

## Why it matters to your business

This is the same full-stack pattern needed for any fleet of high-value machines: reliable capture at the edge, resilient communications, and one interface for immediate operational decisions.
