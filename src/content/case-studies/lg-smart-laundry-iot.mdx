## The engineering challenge

A laundromat chain lives or dies on machine uptime across branches. Owners need to see every washer, sensor, lock, and camera from one screen — and fix problems remotely before sending a technician across town.

## What ELG engineered

ELG built the full digital infrastructure: custom IoT MDB controllers, hardware assembly, on-site configuration, and a cloud platform with a 3D store view, device control, CCTV feeds, and environmental sensing. The platform supports owner, staff, and investor access tiers with remote monitoring and proactive troubleshooting.

## Why it matters to your business

This is the turnkey-ecosystem pattern ELG repeats across industries: custom edge hardware, resilient connectivity, and a dashboard your operations team actually enjoys opening. If it works across laundromat branches, it works across your sites.
