## The engineering challenge

The homeowner wanted everything in one place: adjustable lighting, front-door motion sensing, CCTV, moisture and temperature sensing, water-leak detection, and IR control of the air conditioner — with remote access simple enough for the whole family.

## What ELG engineered

ELG designed, developed, integrated, and installed the complete system from scratch: room-by-room devices, a unifying hub, and a user-friendly dashboard with worldwide smartphone access to lights and climate.

## Why it matters to your business

Smart-building retrofits are integration problems, not gadget problems. This project shows ELG's strength in unifying mixed protocols behind one reliable interface — the same pattern behind our commercial and industrial deployments.
