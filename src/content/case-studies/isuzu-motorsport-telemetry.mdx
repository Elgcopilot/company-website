## The engineering challenge

A national race series needs every car watched at once: speed, RPM, throttle, fuel pressure, and ignition timing — streamed live to race control with zero tolerance for dropouts. Trackside conditions are brutal: heat, vibration, crowded RF spectrum, and decisions that cannot wait for a replay.

## What ELG engineered

ELG built an integrated motorsport ecosystem: high-precision telemetry capture, low-latency live streaming hardware, and a professional analytics platform. The director dashboard shows grid status, per-car traces, a live track map, and an automated breach log so officials can act on limit violations the moment they happen.

## Why it matters to your business

If a system holds sub-second telemetry across a 30-car grid in race heat, it will hold your factory, fleet, or remote site. Motorsport is ELG's harshest proving ground — and this platform passes it every race weekend.
